How we got here

Our story, one step at a time

01
The First Glance
One evening after a long day at work, I went to the rooftop of Bank of Africa in Fort Portal City to clear my mind — and that's when I saw a beautiful young woman walking below. Unlike David and Bathsheba, ours would become a story of love, patience, and God's grace.
02
Right Place, Right Time
I wasn't alone that evening — I was with someone who knew you personally. When he saw I couldn't take my eyes off you, I told him: "I think I've just seen someone I want to spend the rest of my life with." He gave me your contact. God had placed me in the right place at exactly the right time.
03
Winning Your Heart (2024)
You weren't exactly welcoming at first — principled, careful, skeptical of a stranger. Over time we became close friends. You'd change the topic whenever I brought up love. You weren't ready. But I wasn't ready to give up either. You once even stopped me from betting because you genuinely cared about my future — that's when I knew you weren't just beautiful, you wanted me to become a better man. And finally, I was getting a place in your heart.
04
Hope Arrives — 10 August
On my birthday, you sent a message that changed everything: "I asked people who knew you, and I found out you are not a bad person." You mentioned it was unfortunate I'd already left Fort Portal before we could meet. I promised you: "I'll be back... and we'll finally meet."
05
Our First Meeting
I returned to Fort Portal for my parents' celebration, and we finally met at Richard's Sweetness World, Khunga–Bunyonyi. You were even more beautiful in person than the girl I first saw from the rooftop. We talked, we laughed, and every minute made me more certain you were the woman I'd been praying for.
06
The Day Everything Changed — 18 August
The words I'd waited over a year and a half to hear finally came: "Yes... I love you too." That day didn't just begin our relationship — it began a new version of me.
07
A Better Man
You became one of the greatest blessings God has given me. You changed my thinking, my habits, the way I see life. I don't represent only myself anymore — I represent us. We've had misunderstandings and moments that tested us, but through it all, you have never given up on us. Here's to forever. I love you, Ritah.
