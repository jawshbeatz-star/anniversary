For Ritah

My Dearest Mamito,

If you've found this page, then you've discovered the most important part of this website — the part that belongs only to you.

There are many things I tell you every day, but there are also things I want you to read whenever you doubt how much you mean to me.

Thank you for saying "Yes" on that beautiful evening of 18th August. You didn't just become my girlfriend — you became my peace, my motivation, my safe place, and one of God's greatest blessings in my life.

You changed me in ways I never imagined. You taught me patience, responsibility, and what it truly means to love someone. Because of you, I no longer live only for myself. Every decision I make carries your name too, because I represent us.

I know I haven't always been the man you deserve. I'm sorry for every mood swing, every moment my emotions hurt you, every unnecessary argument, and every time I let my anger speak louder than my love.

Most of all, I'm sorry for the photoshoot I ruined. I know how much that day meant to us — our anniversary, birthday, and graduation memories. I can't go back and change what happened, but I carry the regret in my heart every day. Thank you for choosing forgiveness when I gave you every reason to walk away.

You have stood by me through moments when many people would have left. You believed in me, corrected me, encouraged me, and loved me even when I wasn't easy to love.

I don't know exactly what our future will look like, but I know one thing with all my heart: I want every chapter of it to be with you. I want us to build a home filled with laughter, prayer, and peace. I want us to become husband and wife before God. I want us to raise a beautiful family together. I want to travel the world with you, celebrate your victories, hold your hand through every challenge, and grow old beside you.

Right now, I may not have everything I dream of giving you. But I promise that as long as God gives me strength, I will work tirelessly to build a life where you are loved, protected, respected, and cared for. I want to give you beautiful experiences, thoughtful gifts, and a life where you never have to question whether you are cherished.

My greatest dream isn't simply to buy you jewelry, phones, or cars. My greatest dream is to become the kind of husband who makes you feel like the luckiest woman in the world every single day.

One day, when the time is right, I want to stand before God, our families, and everyone we love, take your hands in mine, and call you my bride.

Until that day... I promise to keep choosing you. To keep growing. To keep learning. To keep loving you.

Forever wasn't just a word I told you. It became my decision the day you said "Yes."

I love you yesterday. I love you today. And by God's grace, I'll love you for the rest of my life.

Forever yours,
Jawsh ❤️
